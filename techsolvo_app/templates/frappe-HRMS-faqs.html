Frequently Asked Questions

What is an HRMS, and why do businesses need it?
An HRMS automates HR tasks like payroll, attendance, and employee management, reducing manual work and improving efficiency. Frappe HRMS is open-source, customizable, and cost-effective.
How is Frappe HRMS different from other HRMS solutions?
Unlike traditional HRMS with high costs and vendor lock-in, Frappe HRMS is open-source, highly customizable, and has no per-user fees.
What features does Frappe HRMS offer?
It includes Payroll, Attendance, Leave, Employee Management, Hiring, Performance Tracking, Training, Expense Management, and Analytics.
Is Frappe HRMS suitable for small businesses?
Absolutely! It’s scalable, affordable, and easy to implement, making it ideal for SMEs.
How secure is employee data in Frappe HRMS?
It offers role-based access, encrypted databases, and audit trails for maximum data protection.
